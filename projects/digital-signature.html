Digital Signature Solution

e‑Signature platform for modern document management

I built a practical e‑signature app that feels familiar from day one. You can customize your dashboard, bring in PDFs, make scans searchable with OCR, get quick AI summaries, draw your signature, and drop it exactly where it belongs. Sharing is simple and permissioned, tasks keep everyone aligned, real‑time notifications are delivered via SignalR, and every signature is backed by certificates and strong crypto.
